The Observation Archive contains member-submitted reports documenting recurring symbols, unusual coincidences, dreams, field observations, conversations, locations, and events believed to warrant preservation.

A record may be preserved even when no conclusion has been reached.

Many reports are mundane, some are not.


## Classification Categories

Observation reports are currently organized into several broad categories:

* Symbolic Recurrence
* Geographic Correspondence
* Dream Record
* Audio / Transmission
* Convocation Report
* Site Report
* Unclassified

Categories have changed over time.

Early reports are often inconsistent.

## Submission Guidelines

Members submitting observations are encouraged to include:

* Date
* Location
* Description
* Supporting materials
* Related documents
* Prior occurrences, if known

The Archives Department does not determine meaning.

It preserves recurrence.
