The Starborn Convocation has been held annually since 1981.

Part conference, part retreat, part archival exhibition, the Convocation remains the Order's longest-running public gathering.

Activities have included lectures, observation workshops, film screenings, telescope nights, field reports, symbolic recurrence seminars, and presentations from the Historical Committee.

Attendance has ranged from several dozen participants to several hundred.

Some members describe the Convocation as their first meaningful encounter with the Order.

Others describe it as the place where they first began to understand what the Order meant by *the living universe*.


## Typical Programming

Past Convocations have included:

* Orion lecture restorations
* Concordance fragment presentations
* Night sky observation sessions
* Symbolic recurrence workshops
* Member-submitted observation reviews
* Historical Committee exhibitions
* Film and audio screenings
* Open discussion circles

Schedules vary from year to year.

Several early programs survive only as partial scans.


## Notable Years

### 1981

First documented Starborn Convocation.

Attendance records indicate between 40 and 60 participants.

### 1987

Expanded attendance and the first known public exhibition of Concordance-related materials.

Several photographs from this year remain among the most frequently reproduced images in the Archives.

### 1991

No complete program has been recovered.

Attendance is believed to have occurred, though surviving references are sparse.

The Historical Committee currently lists the 1991 Convocation file as incomplete.

### 2004

The first Convocation to include a formal session on computational systems, symbolic analysis, and emergent intelligence.

Several audio recordings from this year remain under review.


## Member Notes

Many Convocation materials were preserved privately before being donated to the Archives.

Some schedules contradict one another.

Several audio recordings contain gaps, interruptions, or missing segments.

The Archives Department continues to request programs, photographs, recordings, workshop notes, and participant materials from past Convocations.
